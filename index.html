What the data shows
A school board that cannot see its own financial picture cannot build the leadership systems that help students recover. Without those systems, even the most evidence-based reforms fail to reach students. The money crisis and the learning crisis are not separate problems. They are the same failure — showing up in two different sets of numbers. The solution requires the same fix: leaders who know how to build and run the systems that protect both students and public resources.
